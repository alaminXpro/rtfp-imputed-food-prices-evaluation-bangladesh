# A5 — Two-source check of survey noise in Dhaka (TCB vs WFP)

*Generated from `outputs/tables/T_A5_tcb_two_source.csv` (script `analysis/a5_tcb_dhaka.py`).*

**Claim.** For Dhaka Sadar, the reliability of WFP's monthly price changes estimated from an independent second source (TCB daily bulletins) is 0.23–0.54 across items and lentil grades, closer to the naive autocovariance estimates for all markets (Table 1, A_main: 0.27–0.51) than to the trimmed ones (0.67–0.79). Survey monthly changes are noisy; the noise band in Table 1 should be read with its naive end as plausible, not as an extreme.

**Method.** TCB monthly mean of daily (min+max)/2; WFP = RTFP plain column for Dhaka Sadar. Monthly log changes for months where both sources have consecutive months. rel = Cov(dWFP, dTCB) / Var(dWFP), valid if the two measurement errors are independent. TCB does not appear in WFP's HDX source list. CIs: moving-block bootstrap (block 3 months, 2,000 draws).

**Caveats.**
- One market; 20–47 months per item; intervals are wide (rice spans below 0 to above 1).
- TCB's prices span several Dhaka markets and quality grades, so its 'signal' is citywide; market-specific movements in WFP's quote count as noise here. This makes the estimate a lower bound for single-market reliability.
- Item specifications differ (wheat flour: WFP is 22% above TCB loose atta in level). Which masur grade WFP prices is not documented; all three are shown.
