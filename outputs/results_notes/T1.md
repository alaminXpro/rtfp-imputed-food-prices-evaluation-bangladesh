**Method.** Autocovariances of observed local changes at lags 0–3 within markets; naive Var(ν) = −g1, AR(1)+white-noise estimator where identified; reliability = 1 − 2Var(ν)/g0. Gate per methodology D2 with the 2026-10-09 implementation clarification. Bootstrap: 500 two-way (market × month) pigeonhole draws, the same draw for noise sample and pairs.
